# Supported Modules

The recorder lists every active module whose id starts with `dxcufgbs-` or whose author is Dxcufgb, plus any other module that [registered with the API](API). All of the modules below also **report state**: they send the recorder a snapshot of their own state and leave breadcrumbs of their main actions while a recording runs. Each module's wiki has a *Debug Recorder integration* page with exactly what it sends; none of them send names, free text, image paths or URLs.
